    # Atticus Control Plane Demonstration and Portfolio Specification

    ## Purpose

    The demonstration proves the most important engineering claims of Atticus Control Plane. Every visible result is live, replayed from a signed real trace, cached from a dated artifact, or clearly marked illustrative.

    ## Signature story

    A visitor asks Atticus to analyze a macroeconomic question and its implications for a synthetic institution. Atticus visibly routes to Atlas and FedLens, builds a typed scenario, runs BalanceLab, requests EvalForge validation, and returns a cited report plus a readable operational trace.

    ## Modes

    - Guided replay from a signed real trace.
- Quota-limited live run over public and synthetic data.
- Architecture mode showing plan, policy, evidence, cost, latency, and evaluation.
- Private local-runner demonstration only as a recorded or owner-controlled session.

    ## Guided sequence

    - Introduce the laboratory and visitor objective.
- Show a concise operational plan.
- Display catalog and policy decision.
- Animate specialist calls in tmux-inspired panes.
- Render evidence and deterministic artifacts as they become valid.
- Show EvalForge results and failure slices.
- Deliver the cited report with limitations.
- Open one failure and its regression test.

    ## Safety and boundedness

    - Fixed public catalog and synthetic/public data.
- No external writes or private/local tools.
- Strict time, token, spend, and concurrency quotas.
- No hidden reasoning, secrets, or raw private traces.
- Replays identify date, code, model, config, and signature.

    ## Measurements shown

    - Step and terminal status.
- Policy/approval outcomes.
- Selected specialist/tool and version.
- Latency, cost estimate, and quota usage.
- Evidence count and citation support.
- Calculation artifact digest and reconciliation.
- Evaluation result and known limitations.

    ## Failure and fallback

    - Truthful GPU startup state offers replay.
- Unavailable specialist yields approved cached artifact or explicit degraded result.
- Budget exhaustion stops cleanly with partial work.
- Failed evaluation remains visible and links to the failure museum.

    The demo remains keyboard-operable, useful on mobile, compatible with reduced motion, and understandable without sound. A short visual loop, 90-second tour, and technical walkthrough all derive from the same truthful evidence.
