    # Atlas Demonstration and Portfolio Specification

    ## Purpose

    The demonstration proves the most important engineering claims of Atlas. Every visible result is live, replayed from a signed real trace, cached from a dated artifact, or clearly marked illustrative.

    ## Signature story

    A visitor asks what changed in the inflation outlook over a historical window. Atlas shows what data and policy documents were available at each point, identifies support and contradiction, computes reproducible charts, and returns a cited snapshot instead of a timeless summary.

    ## Modes

    - Signed historical replay.
- Live query over a controlled public snapshot.
- Source and lineage explorer.
- Temporal “what was known then?” comparison.

    ## Guided sequence

    - Set question and as-of range.
- Inspect eligible sources.
- Watch retrieval and evidence accumulation.
- View deterministic chart.
- Compare supporting and contradicting evidence.
- Open citations and lineage.
- Download snapshot manifest.
- Inspect a temporal failure example.

    ## Safety and boundedness

    - Approved source registry only.
- No arbitrary crawl or financial advice.
- Query and compute limits.
- Rights status displayed.
- Every snapshot date-stamped.

    ## Measurements shown

    - Source and release versions.
- Temporal eligibility.
- Retrieval/evidence coverage.
- Citation support.
- Calculation digest.
- Latency, cost, freshness, and connector health.

    ## Failure and fallback

    - Source outage uses last approved snapshot and labels staleness.
- Insufficient evidence produces an unresolved answer.
- Restricted content shows metadata/source link only.
- Failed calculations are never replaced by model-generated numbers.

    The demo remains keyboard-operable, useful on mobile, compatible with reduced motion, and understandable without sound. A short visual loop, 90-second tour, and technical walkthrough all derive from the same truthful evidence.
