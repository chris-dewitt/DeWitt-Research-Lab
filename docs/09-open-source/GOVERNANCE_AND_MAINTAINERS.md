# Governance, Maintainer Ladder, and Research Independence

## Model

DRL uses director-led, benevolent-dictator governance. DeWitt has final authority over mission, brand, major architecture, official releases, research publication, and maintainer appointment. Authority is delegated by component and can be revoked for cause or lack of capacity. The governance model is explicit so contributors understand how decisions are made.

## Ladder

- contributor;
- regular contributor;
- reviewer;
- component maintainer;
- laboratory maintainer;
- research lead or official DRL researcher;
- director.

Promotion considers sustained quality, review judgment, security/data responsibility, collaboration, documentation, mentorship, and alignment with the open research charter. Commit count, employment status, sponsorship, or personal closeness does not confer authority.

## Decision classes

- **Routine implementation:** component maintainer or assigned reviewer under existing requirements.
- **Public API, protocol, model/data release, architecture, or license:** ADR plus required reviewers and director approval.
- **Security incident containment:** designated operator may act immediately and document afterward.
- **Research conclusion:** authors retain scholarly responsibility; methods, funding, and disagreements are disclosed.
- **Roadmap:** director after public input and evidence review.
- **Trademark and official status:** director under trademark policy.

## Open-development expectations

Major proposals, ADRs, roadmaps, and release evidence are public unless privacy, security, legal, or contractual obligations require restriction. Decisions should occur in durable repository artifacts rather than private chat whenever possible. Private discussion can inform a decision but cannot replace its public rationale.

## Sponsors and partners

Sponsors may suggest problems, provide infrastructure, fund approved work, and receive acknowledgment. They do not purchase benchmark outcomes, publication conclusions, undisclosed access, maintainer status, or roadmap control. Conflicts and funding are disclosed in affected research.

## Maintainer obligations

Maintainers are expected to:

- protect users and contributor data;
- review within stated capacity;
- enforce license/provenance and evidence requirements;
- preserve compatibility or document migrations;
- disclose conflicts;
- contribute fixes upstream where appropriate;
- hand off ownership before departure where possible;
- avoid representing personal opinion as an official DRL decision.

## Disagreement and appeals

Technical disagreement is documented with alternatives and evidence. A contributor may request review by another maintainer or the director. The director's final decision is recorded. Research papers may document minority interpretations when scientifically relevant.

## Community councils

DRL may form nonbinding advisory groups for accessibility, teaching, open models, security, or domain research. Councils provide expertise and public input; they do not supersede director-led governance unless this document is formally amended.
