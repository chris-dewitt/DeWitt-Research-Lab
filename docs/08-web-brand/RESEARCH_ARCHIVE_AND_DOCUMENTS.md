# Research Archive and Controlled Document Experience

## Content types

- working paper;
- technical report;
- benchmark/model/data report;
- architecture note;
- experiment note;
- teaching note;
- release note;
- failure report.

## Document chrome

Display DRL ID, title, authors, abstract, status, revision, date, citation, license, related code/data/model, replication status, and change history.

Research document view supports reading mode, margin outline, footnotes/citations, figures/tables, code, downloadable source, and machine-readable metadata.

Draft/review documents are labeled and not indexed as finished publications. Superseded documents point to replacement.
