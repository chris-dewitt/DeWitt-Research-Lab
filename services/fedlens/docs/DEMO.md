    # FedLens Demonstration and Portfolio Specification

    ## Purpose

    The demonstration proves the most important engineering claims of FedLens. Every visible result is live, replayed from a signed real trace, cached from a dated artifact, or clearly marked illustrative.

    ## Signature story

    A visitor selects two FOMC statements. FedLens shows exact insertions and removals, source-bound semantic shifts, a policy timeline, and a predeclared market event window while distinguishing direct evidence, model annotation, and interpretation.

    ## Modes

    - Side-by-side policy-document microscope.
- Speaker and theme timeline.
- Signed reproducible event-study replay.
- Failure example showing why a naive sentiment score failed.

    ## Guided sequence

    - Choose meetings and verify exact versions.
- View lexical differences.
- Inspect semantic annotations and confidence.
- Explore source spans and policy timeline.
- Run or replay the declared event window.
- Open methods and replication manifest.
- Inspect annotator disagreement.
- See linked regression tests.

    ## Safety and boundedness

    - Official approved sources only.
- No investment advice.
- Exact source text cannot be replaced by generation.
- Event studies are labeled noncausal unless the design supports more.
- Public queries and compute are bounded.

    ## Measurements shown

    - Version and timestamp integrity.
- Alignment quality.
- Retrieval and citation support.
- Annotation confidence and agreement.
- Event-study artifact digest.
- Latency, cost, corpus freshness, and source health.

    ## Failure and fallback

    - Unavailable source version causes a refusal, not substitution.
- Low-confidence alignment falls back to exact raw diff.
- Missing market data omits the event result.
- Signed examples remain available if live services are cold.

    The demo remains keyboard-operable, useful on mobile, compatible with reduced motion, and understandable without sound. A short visual loop, 90-second tour, and technical walkthrough all derive from the same truthful evidence.
