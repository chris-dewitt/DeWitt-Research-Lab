# Portfolio and Research Content Requirements

Every major project page must answer:

1. What human or research problem does this solve?
2. Why is AI needed, and where is deterministic software better?
3. What is the architecture and trust boundary?
4. What data and licenses are involved?
5. How is quality measured?
6. What failed and changed?
7. How can a visitor run or replay it?
8. How can a contributor help?
9. What is the current maturity?
10. What evidence supports every displayed metric?

## Required project assets

- concise overview;
- architecture diagram;
- signature workflow;
- live/replay demo;
- evaluation report;
- security/privacy note;
- data/model card links;
- API/package install path;
- known limitations;
- failure museum entry;
- technical article or research note;
- repository and contribution links;
- 20-second loop, 90-second demo, and technical walkthrough plan.
