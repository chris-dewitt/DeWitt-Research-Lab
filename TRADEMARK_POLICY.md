# DeWitt Research Laboratory Trademark Policy

## Purpose

Open licensing grants rights in code and other designated artifacts; it does not grant the right to create confusion about who operates DRL or which releases are official.

## Reserved identifiers

DeWitt Research Laboratory, DRL when used as the laboratory identifier, Atticus product/model names, official logos, seals, badges, domain names, and visual source marks are reserved unless explicitly licensed.

## Allowed nominative use

You may accurately say that software is based on, compatible with, forked from, or evaluated using DRL projects. Preserve required notices and link to the official project. Do not alter screenshots or results to imply endorsement.

## Permission required

Permission is required to:

- name a product, organization, hosted service, domain, conference, or model in a confusingly similar way;
- use official logos as your primary identity;
- call a fork or hosted instance “official,” “certified,” or “approved”; or
- represent yourself as a DRL researcher, maintainer, partner, or agent without appointment.

## Community forks

Forks should choose a distinct name and clearly state modifications, maintenance responsibility, and non-affiliation. Compatibility badges, if created, will have published tests and usage terms.

## Reporting misuse

Use the contact mechanism published in the official repository/site. Do not place personal contact details in generated templates until the Director chooses them.
