# Commercial Sustainability Without Compromising the Mission

## Principle

DRL’s public mission and open-source commitments are not loss leaders to be withdrawn after adoption. Commercial activity funds maintenance, research, teaching, and access while keeping public licenses, scientific conclusions, and user rights intact.

## Service ladder

1. **Free public goods:** source, benchmark code, selected datasets, model releases, documentation, teaching materials, limited public sandbox.
2. **Paid learning:** workshops, cohort training, institutional curricula, technical seminars.
3. **Professional services:** consulting, research design, evaluations, governance, custom integrations, deployment hardening.
4. **Managed operation:** hosted Atticus/DRL services, support, upgrades, monitoring, private environments, compliance evidence.
5. **Custom research artifacts:** client-owned adapters, evaluation suites, connectors, and reports under contracts consistent with upstream licenses and privacy.

## Guardrails

- No sponsor or customer controls public research conclusions or roadmap.
- Public benchmark scores are not pay-to-play.
- Private work is separated from public datasets and model releases unless the owner grants explicit rights.
- Paid tiers may provide capacity, service levels, private integrations, and support—not fabricated safety or correctness claims.
- Costs, conflicts, and sponsorship relevant to research are disclosed.

## Business validation before scale

Track demand through contributor activity, deployment requests, workshops, consulting inquiries, and public usage. Do not buy persistent GPU capacity or establish enterprise commitments before measured need and operational maturity. Every paid offering requires owner, support boundary, cost model, terms, security model, and exit/portability plan.
