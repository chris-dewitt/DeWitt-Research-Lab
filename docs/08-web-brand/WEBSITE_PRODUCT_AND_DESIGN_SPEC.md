# Personal Academic Portfolio Product and Design Specification

## Product definition

`https://www.dewitt-labs.com` is Christopher Noxon DeWitt's personal academic
portfolio. It presents his current education, intellectual interests,
engineering work, independent research, selected projects, and intended
progression toward graduate work in computer science.

The repository may retain DeWitt Research Workshop/DRL identifiers internally.
The public site does not present those identifiers as an institution or brand.

## Primary story

1. Christopher is currently a student in the Master of Applied Data Science
   program at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill.
2. He engineers complex systems at work and studies them part time.
3. His portfolio explores artificial intelligence, agent systems, evidence,
   evaluation, deterministic tools, and related complex-system questions.
4. He hopes to continue from data science toward graduate work in computer
   science.

## Audience

Faculty, prospective advisers or admissions readers, research peers, and
relevant employers who want a clear picture of Christopher's background,
interests, trajectory, and ability to build. Developers may inspect the code,
but contribution is not the site's purpose.

## Required routes

- Home
- Research
- Projects
- About

## Selected evidence

`TR-2026-001`, recorded success/degraded runs, source code, architecture,
evaluation, and negative results can appear as selected research or project
evidence. None outranks the personal introduction or turns the site into a
laboratory homepage.

## Design

Use the existing cream-on-black research-terminal influence with simpler
composition, readable typography, and fewer panels. No fake status, laboratory
map, institutional seal, department language, or Atticus navigation layer.

## Boundaries

- No unverified claim that independent work is UNC-Chapel Hill coursework,
  research, sponsorship, or endorsement.
- No employer name or confidential/proprietary material.
- No fictional staff, collaborators, laboratory, institute, or public service.
- No chat or live backend required.
- Every project state and evidence claim remains accurate.

## Success criteria

In under one minute a visitor can state Christopher's current degree, what kinds
of systems interest him, what he has built, and that he hopes to move toward
graduate computer-science study. The visitor can then reach selected research,
projects, source, CV/contact, and honest limitations.
