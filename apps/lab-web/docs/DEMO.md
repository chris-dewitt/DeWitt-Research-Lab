# Workshop Web Demonstration and Portfolio Specification

## Purpose

The demonstration proves the workshop's most important current engineering
claim: the five prototype projects compose into a bounded, inspectable local
workflow with evidence, deterministic calculations, policy, and evaluation.
Every visible result is live, replayed from a signed trace, cached from a dated
artifact, or clearly marked illustrative or planned.

## Signature story

A visitor enters through the academic workshop homepage, selects **Watch a
recorded run**, and follows one integrated workflow across Atticus, Atlas,
FedLens, BalanceLab AI, and EvalForge. The visitor can switch from success to
degraded evidence, then read `TR-2026-001` for the method and limitations.

The experience resembles a disciplined financial research terminal and `tmux`
workspace while remaining readable, accessible, and truthful.

## V1 modes

- Static portfolio and report exploration.
- Signed success and degraded replay.
- Mobile transcript and desktop multi-pane evidence workspace.
- Future bounded live Atticus only after its separate release gates pass.

## Guided sequence

1. Read the workshop thesis.
2. Watch the success replay.
3. Switch to the degraded replay and compare what changed.
4. Inspect specialists, policy, evidence, calculations, and evaluation.
5. Read `TR-2026-001`, methods, limitations, and source.
6. Review the Stage-B no-winner result and six reasons.
7. Continue to a project, local quickstart, founder context, or contact.

## Safety and boundedness

- Live, replay, fixture, and planned states are labeled.
- Only public and synthetic data appear.
- Demo-HMAC signing is not presented as production identity.
- No fake metrics, activity, staff, or government/university implication.
- Public tools remain unavailable until bounded and approved.
- Static content and transcript fallbacks always work.

## Measurements shown

- artifact identity, version, and verification date;
- task and evidence state;
- evaluation result and limitation;
- project maturity;
- cost and latency only when measured and useful;
- source, report, and reproduction links.

## Failure and fallback

- a broken viewer exposes transcript, manifest, and source;
- a cold or absent backend offers recorded evidence;
- verification failure cannot be rendered as a successful run;
- no endless loading or fabricated processing;
- every evidence page returns to ordinary navigation.
