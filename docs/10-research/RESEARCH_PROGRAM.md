# Research Program, Papers, and Replication

## Current Director-approved academic program

The current portfolio research program is **Computational Finance of
Intelligence**, approved in RES-017 and specified in
`COMPUTATIONAL_FINANCE_OF_INTELLIGENCE.md`.

It uses Belief Diffusion as a shared methods bridge for three paper tracks:

1. optimal stopping and the option value of thinking;
2. linguistic framing, derivative valuation, and no-arbitrage belief repair;
3. market-based aggregation of human and machine beliefs.

This program extends the initial research lines below. It does not replace the
Atticus, evaluation, temporal-research, deterministic-tool, or human-agency
work already approved.

## Initial research lines

1. Safe open-weight tool orchestration and permission behavior.
2. AtticusBench and trajectory-level evaluation.
3. Core-to-Edge distillation and calibrated escalation.
4. Temporal RAG and source-to-claim evidence for economic research.
5. Federal Reserve language change and reproducible event studies.
6. Deterministic quantitative tools with LLM explanation.
7. Human approval UX and agent trust.
8. Evaluation judge calibration and failure analysis.

## Publication forms

- working paper;
- system/technical report;
- benchmark report;
- model/data card;
- experiment note;
- replication package;
- teaching paper/tutorial.

## Standards

- clear research question and contribution;
- related work and source citations;
- methods and preregistered primary metrics where feasible;
- data/license/ethics;
- reproducible code/environment;
- uncertainty and limitations;
- negative/null results where informative;
- funding/conflict disclosure;
- authorship criteria.
