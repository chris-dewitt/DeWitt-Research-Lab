    # BalanceLab AI Demonstration and Portfolio Specification

    ## Purpose

    The demonstration proves the most important engineering claims of BalanceLab AI. Every visible result is live, replayed from a signed real trace, cached from a dated artifact, or clearly marked illustrative.

    ## Signature story

    A visitor loads a synthetic regional bank, shapes a bear-steepener and deposit-beta stress, watches the deterministic engine reconcile balances and compute NII and risk drivers, then asks Atticus for an explanation that links every number to the calculation artifact.

    ## Modes

    - Interactive sample institution.
- Curated signed scenario replay.
- Calculation audit and method view.
- Scenario comparison.
- Failure case where reconciliation blocks a misleading result.

    ## Guided sequence

    - Select a sample institution.
- Inspect balance sheet and methods.
- Adjust curve and behavior controls.
- Validate and confirm scenario.
- Execute and reconcile.
- Explore driver charts and tables.
- Read artifact-bound Atticus explanation.
- Download reproducibility manifest.

    ## Safety and boundedness

    - Synthetic data by default.
- No financial advice.
- Deterministic engine is authoritative.
- Bounded inputs and isolated uploads.
- Explanation cannot calculate or mutate results.
- Limitations are prominent.

    ## Measurements shown

    - Engine and method version.
- Scenario assumptions and confirmations.
- Run time and reconciliation.
- Key metric deltas and driver decomposition.
- Artifact digest.
- Explanation faithfulness and evaluation status.
- Cost and resource use.

    ## Failure and fallback

    - Signed replay remains if engine is unavailable.
- Invalid scenarios show actionable field errors.
- Reconciliation or numerical failure stops report generation.
- Model explanation failure leaves calculation results intact and labeled unexplained.

    The demo remains keyboard-operable, useful on mobile, compatible with reduced motion, and understandable without sound. A short visual loop, 90-second tour, and technical walkthrough all derive from the same truthful evidence.
