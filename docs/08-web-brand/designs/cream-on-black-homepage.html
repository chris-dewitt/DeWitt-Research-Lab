SECTION / LABORATORY

Laboratory thesis

Atticus is the open-weight guide and operator. Specialist systems own research, policy analysis, deterministic modeling, and evaluation. Open models and open software are institutional commitments. Safety, traceability, evaluation, and human agency matter as much as capability.

DeWitt Research Laboratory is an independent initiative: one researcher building in public. It is not a university, company research division, government laboratory, or fictional staff organization. Ambition is institutional; present operation is personal, practical, and open to serious collaborators.
