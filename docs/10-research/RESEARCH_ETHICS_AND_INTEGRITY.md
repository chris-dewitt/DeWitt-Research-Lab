# Research Ethics and Integrity

DRL will not fabricate results, cherry-pick benchmarks, hide material failures, misrepresent synthetic data as human data, imply causal findings from descriptive analysis, or use private/employer data.

Human interaction research, trace donation, or user studies require informed consent proportionate to risk. Publicly available data is not automatically ethically unrestricted. Sensitive groups and downstream harms are considered.

Corrections, withdrawals, and replication failures are published promptly. Sponsors and tool/model providers are disclosed when relevant. Generated text may assist drafting but authors remain responsible for claims and citations.
